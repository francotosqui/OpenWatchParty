# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenWatchParty welcomes contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to make participation in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Offer constructive feedback and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and people's time.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you encounter unacceptable behavior in an issue, pull request, or comment, use GitHub's **Report content** menu and choose **Report to repository admins** when available. You can also [report abuse to GitHub Support](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). For other concerns, open an issue asking for a private contact channel **without including personal or sensitive details**.

The maintainer will review reports, protect the privacy of people involved where possible, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation as appropriate. Reports will be handled fairly and without retaliation.

This code applies to project spaces and to behavior that affects the project community.
